## Facts read from the object, not invented

- Title: ANDO OKWA LI IHE TO SHANGA OPO WAA DHIMBWE
- Subtitle on cover: Omuthigululwakalo gwAawambo ohela nonena
- Author on cover: Petrus Angula Mbenzi
- Year: 2021
- Publisher: Namtranslations Services cc, PO Box 2021, Windhoek
- Printed by: Microwide Publishing Press
- ISBN: 9789994552320
- Illustrators (colophon): Max Shimi and Kashindi Asuiku
- Cover: homestead plan with numbered places, woman carrying a clay pot, compass rose

## What not to publish

The zip also has full contents pages ii–ix and three pages of *Oontekelohapu*.
Those are the book. Do not OCR them onto the public site.
Do not add a page-turn through every contents photo.
Three look-inside pages is enough to put a smile on the author's face.

## Suggested motion (keep it quiet)

- Bookstore card: cover sits on the clay panel. Hover lifts 4px, no bounce.
- Book page: CSS 3D flip, front → back. Buttons: Cover / Back / Look inside.
- Look inside: three pages, fade or a short page-turn. Caption under each.
- Homestead map: the cover drawing as a still. Later, numbered hotspots to the five homestead stories already in the catalogue. Do not invent the other nine places.
- Prefers `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` → no flip, static images.
