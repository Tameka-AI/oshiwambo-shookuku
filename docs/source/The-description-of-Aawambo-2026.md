# The description of Aawambo 2026

Aawambo are the Bantu speaking people who live in Northern Namibia and Southern Angola. According to the official 2023 Population and Housing Census Main Report released by the Namibia Statistics Agency, the Aawambo make up approximately 50% of Namibia's total of the They speak language cluster that is known as Oshiwambo. Oshiwambo consists of fourteen mutually intelligible dialects, namely Oshindonga, Oshikwanyama, Oshikwambi, Oshingandjera, Oshikwaluudhi, Oshikolonkadhi, Oshimbalantu, Oshiunda, Oshimbadja, Oshikwankwa, Oshivale, Oshindongona, Oshikafima, and Oshindombodhola.
